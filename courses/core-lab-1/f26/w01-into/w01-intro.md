<bold-title>Course Description</bold-title>
In this course, you will be introduced to the fundamental techniques in digital photography, concentrating on digital cameras, file management and inkjet printing.This hands-on technical course supports the development of your creative and professional working methods providing you with a thorough understanding of Lightroom and beginning Photoshop with an in-depth look into camera equipment and imaging software. This lab course runs in tandem with Core Studio 1.

<bold-title>What will we learn?</bold-title>

<v-clicks>

- Digital workflow and file management

<bold-title>Learning Outcomes</bold-title>

By the end of the semester, students will be able to:

<v-clicks>

- Show an introductory understanding of fundamental technical processes of digital photography, including <u>capture, file formats, file management and raw processing</u>
- Show an introductory working knowledge of production and <u>digital darkroom workflow</u> and processes
- Show an understanding of an ability to use post-production to edit images for various output methods, including <u>digital chromogenic and inkjet printing</u>
- Show an understanding of <u>color correction and management</u>
- Competently present and discuss work in <u>critiques and classroom discussions</u>
- Be able to archive and document work that is printed, on screen, or time-based in a reflective and representative manner for the <u>learning portfolio</u>
- Show an understanding of <u>environmental impact</u> of photographic production 

</v-clicks>
