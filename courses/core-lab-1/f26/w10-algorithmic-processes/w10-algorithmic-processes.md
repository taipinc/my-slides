<bold-title>What is an algorithm?</bold-title>

<v-clicks>

Algorithms are mathematical procedures that have been **crafted by people** — mathematicians and computer engineers who have produced them through years of research.

Every aspect of these procedure have been **studies and carfted by humans**.

Their processes are **transparent**, their results **predictable**, and we **control** the outcome.

<bold-title>Genertive AI and Machine Learning</bold-title>

<v-clicks>

Generative AI are algorithmic processes that uses machine learning to generate text, images, videos, and other data.

Machine learning is research field within Artificial Intelligence (AI), that lets computer learn through patterns in existing data, to be able to generate new, similar, data.

<bold-title>Genertive AI and Machine Learning</bold-title>

<v-clicks>

Machine Learning (ML) is researched, developed, and deployed by humans beings. 

But while the main architecture of ML models is being designed by humans, the values of a neural network are "learned" by a computer, and remains elusive from human understanding.

Furthermore, with proprietary models being obscured as intellectual property, we have no ways of understanding how they work.

Many ML models are tasked with analyzing images and texts. **Generative AI** are ML models that are designed to generate new data - such as new images and texts.

</v-clicks>

<bold-title>What is the difference between an algorithmic an a generative process?</bold-title>

::left::

<ul>
  <li v-click="1">

  An algorithmic process was **defined completely** by humans

  </li>
  <li v-click="3">

  **Processed locally**, on our computer

  </li>
  <li v-click="5">

  Results are more **predictable**
  
  </li>
  <li v-click="7">

  User has **more control** over results

  </li>
  <li v-click="9">

  Our interaction is being kept private

  </li>
</ul>



::right::

<ul>
  <li v-click="2">

   A generative process is based on **machine learning**

  </li>
  <li v-click="4">

  Processed, most likely, in **the cloud**

  </li>
  <li v-click="6">

  Results are **unexpected**
  
  </li>
  <li v-click="8">

  User has **no control** over results

  </li>
  <li v-click="10">

  Our interactions are being sourced for future **machine learning**

  </li>
</ul>
