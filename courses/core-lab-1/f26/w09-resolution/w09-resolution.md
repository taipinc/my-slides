<bold-title>What is Resolution?</bold-title>

<v-click>
    Measurement of How Detailed an Image Is
</v-click>

---
layout: image-caption
image: ./images/Resolution.webp
---

Digital photographs are natively measured in Pixels

---
layout: image-caption
image: ./images/Resolution-2.webp
---

Resolution (or PPI) relates pixel dimensions to physical print dimensions

---
layout: center
---

<bold-title>Resolution: 72 PPI</bold-title>

<v-click>
*In some cases you'll see "DPI" used
</v-click>

---
layout: center
---

<bold-title>PPI = Pixels per Inch</bold-title>

<v-clicks>

- 1770 x 2086 px
- 72 PPI
- 1770 px / 72 ppi ≈ 24.6 inches
- 2086 px / 72 ppi ≈ 29 inches
- 24.6" x 29"

</v-clicks>

---
layout: center
---

<bold-title>Common PPI Values</bold-title>

<v-clicks>

  - Historical display convention: 72 ppi
  - Modern high-resolution display: ~150-250 ppi or higher

</v-clicks>

---
layout: center
---

<bold-title>Print Resolution</bold-title>

<v-click>

For printing, 300 ppi is considered baseline resolution

</v-click>

---
layout: center
---

<bold-title>Don't let conventions determine your artwork!</bold-title>

<v-clicks>

- 300 ppi is a **baseline**, not a requirement.
- You might use 150 ppi or lower, depending on the image and viewing distance.

</v-clicks>

---
layout: center
---

<bold-title>Factors to consider for print size</bold-title>

<v-clicks>

- The **detail captured in the image**: **is it sharp or blurry?**
- The **expected viewing distance**: larger prints are often viewed from farther away.

</v-clicks>

---
layout: center
---

<bold-title>Techniques to improve perceived image detail</bold-title>

<v-clicks>

- Adding artificial noise to soften the appearance of pixelation
- AI-based enlargement: **Generative Upscale**

</v-clicks>

<v-clicks>

- When scanning film, we convert teeny-tiny photographs into digital images
- Enlarging these frames requires higher scanning resolutions

</v-clicks>

<bold-title>Common Scanning Resolutions</bold-title>

35mm frame: 36mm x 24mm (1.4" x 0.94")

<v-clicks>

- 1200ppi : 1,680 x 1,128 px
- 2400ppi : 3,360 x 2,256 px
- 3600ppi : 5,040 x 3,384 px

Note: scanning above a scanner's optical resolution adds pixels, not captured detail.
</v-clicks>
