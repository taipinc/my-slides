- **Exposure Triangle**: The relationship between ISO, shutter speed, and aperture that determines the overall exposure of an image.

- **Stop**: A unit of measurement for exposure, representing a doubling or halving of the amount of light reaching the sensor.

- **Dynamic Range**: The range of light intensities a camera sensor can capture, from the darkest shadows to the brightest highlights.
