<bold-title>Exposure Modes</bold-title>


Exposure modes are different ways to control the camera's exposure, while prioritizing different aspects of the exposure.

<v-clicks>

- (M) **Manual Mode**: the photographer manually controls both shutter and aperture.
- (Av) **Aperture Value Mode**: the photographer sets the aperture, camera determines the shutter speed.
  - In other cameras, this will be called <u>(A) Aperture Priority</u>.
- (Tv) **Time Value Mode**: the photographer sets the shutter speed, camera determines the aperture.
  - In other cameras, this will be called <u>(S) Shutter Priority</u>.
- (P) **Program Mode**: the camera determines both shutter and aperture, and the photographer can shuffle between combinations that yield the same exposure.

</v-clicks>

---
layout: default
---

<bold-title>Exposure Modes</bold-title>

In other words:

<v-clicks>

- Choose (M) **Manual Mode** if you want full control over the exposure.
- Choose (Av) **Aperture Value Mode** if you want to control the depth of field.
- Choose (Tv) **Time Value Mode** if you want to control motion blur.
- Choose (P) **Program Mode** if you want the camera to do the work for you.

Remember!
For a full manual mode, you also want to set the **ISO manually**, otherwise the camera will adjust it automatically.

</v-clicks>
