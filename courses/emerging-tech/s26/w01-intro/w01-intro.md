<br><br>
<v-click>

<span v-mark.underline.red="1">

# Indexicality

</span>

</v-click>

<v-click>
The inherent quality of a photograph to be a direct trace or imprint of the physical world.
<br><br>
The indexical character of photographs encourages interpreters to treat them as ‘objective’ and transparent records of ‘reality’.
</v-click>

<!--
We are used to think about photography, or of a photograph, as being indexical to something in the world. This assumption could be argued about, of course, but generally photographers agree that a photograph is a record of light reflected from an object and projected onto a light-sensitive plane.

Here, we have an image, looking like a photograph, and light played absolutely no part in its creation. Yet, this image might be circulated online, understood by people as a "photograph" - meaning, indexical, and true.
